Reading this comparison

Trip matching

Exact trip ID, service date and start time, after removing BMC’s gt:nmbssncb: namespace. Missing dates or times stay empty. Different identifiers are not guessed to be the same train.

What counts as a delay

A positive, explicit arrival, departure or trip-level delay, in seconds. A trip’s headline value is its largest reported delay, including past stops. Cancelled trips and skipped or NO_DATA stops are excluded from delay counts. Missing delay fields stay unknown; no delays are derived from timestamps.

Snapshot timing

Both feeds are fetched concurrently for each report. Different publication times can explain discrepancies. Feed timestamps older than five minutes are flagged. The report refreshes daily at 07:00, 08:00, 09:00, 17:00, 18:00, 19:00 and 20:00 Europe/Brussels; GitHub may delay scheduled runs.
